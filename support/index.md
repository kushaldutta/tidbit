## Common questions

### Sign in and accounts

- **Sign in with Apple** and **email/password** are both supported.
- If you sign up with email, check your inbox for a verification link before signing in.
- **Forgot password?** Use the reset flow on the login screen, or email us if you're stuck.

### Premium subscriptions

Tidbit Premium is billed through your **Apple ID**.

- **Restore purchases:** Open the Premium paywall → tap **Restore purchases**.
- **Manage or cancel:** iPhone **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions**, or use **Manage subscription** on the paywall.
- **Refunds:** Handled by Apple — request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

We do not store your payment card details.

### Delete my account

You can permanently delete your account in the app:

**Settings → Delete Account**

This removes your account and associated data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

### Class groups, shared decks, and chat

- Join classes during onboarding or from **My Classes** on the home screen.
- Shared decks and class feeds are visible to members of that class.
- **Report** content: long-press a group chat message → Report.
- **Block** a user: long-press a group chat message → Block.

### Notifications

- Enable or adjust notifications in **Settings**.
- If notifications stop, check iOS **Settings → Tidbit → Notifications** and ensure permissions are allowed.

### Privacy

See our [Privacy Policy](/tidbit/privacy/) for what data we collect and how we use it.
